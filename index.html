Report an ad

If an ad shown in Pixel Dispatch is inappropriate, misleading, or not suitable for a 4+ audience, report it and it will be raised with the ad network and blocked.

Email trayyldz@gmail.com with the subject “inappropriate ad”, and include what you can:

  • A screenshot or screen recording of the ad
  • Roughly when you saw it, and your country
  • Where it appeared — between levels, or a video you chose to watch
  • The advertiser or product name, if it was visible

Purchases and restoring

Every level can be finished without spending anything. Gems and Color Bombs are earned by playing; they are also sold, along with a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Changed device or reinstalled? Open Settings in the game (the gear, top right) and tap Restore Purchases. Non-consumable purchases such as Remove Ads come back; refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Progress, settings and your wallet are stored on your iPhone only — there is no account and no server of ours. Deleting the app deletes them. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sends gameplay analytics to Google Analytics for Firebase; purchases go through Apple. Everything that is collected, and how to change your choices, is in the privacy policy.

In the game, Settings → Privacy Choices lets you change your ad consent and tracking answers at any time.
