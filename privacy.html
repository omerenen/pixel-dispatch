Pixel Dispatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Short version. Pixel Dispatch has no account and never asks for your name or email. Your progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sends gameplay analytics to Google Analytics for Firebase. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking, and you can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy Choices.

Who we are

Pixel Dispatch is made and published by Omer Enen, an independent developer ("we"). Questions about this policy or your data: trayyldz@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Your level, gems, Color Bombs, settings, daily-streak state and whether you bought Remove Ads are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The game is funded by full-screen ads between levels and by optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. These are served by Google AdMob, which collects on its own behalf:

  • device identifiers — the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, otherwise identifiers that are specific to this app or to Google;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, iOS version, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, video completion) and diagnostic data about ad loading and crashes.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same one, and to prevent fraud. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalised using activity from other companies' apps and websites.

Your consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears at first launch. On every iPhone, Apple's "Allow to track?" request appears once. Saying no changes nothing in the game.

More: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

To see where levels are too hard, where players stop, and whether the game's economy is fair, the app sends events such as: level started, won, lost or quit, time spent, boosters and gems used, offers shown and purchases completed (the product, never payment details), along with device model, iOS version, app version, and country or approximate location from your IP address.

Firebase links these events with an app-instance identifier it creates; if you have allowed tracking, Google may also associate them with the advertising identifier. We use analytics only to improve the game. Google Analytics keeps event data for the retention period set on our account, at most 14 months.

Purchases — Apple

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app is told only which product was bought. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Game Center — Apple

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and scores are stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Declining Game Center leaves the game fully playable.

Notifications

After you claim your first daily reward, the game asks whether it may send one daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your iPhone; there is no push server and nothing is sent to us.

Who receives data

Only Google (AdMob and Firebase, as described above) and Apple. We do not run a server of our own and do not pass your information to anyone else.

Your choices and rights

  • Privacy Choices in the game's Settings (the gear) opens Google's consent options where they apply, and otherwise this app's page in iOS Settings, where the Allow Tracking switch lives.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls tracking for every app.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we will help; because there is no account, we may need details from you to find the data that belongs to your device.

Children

Pixel Dispatch is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or the age that applies in your country. If you believe a child's information has been collected, email us and we will ask Google to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
